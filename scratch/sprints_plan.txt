0. Where We Are & Plan Logic
Both repos are feature-complete but unproven: the UI has run only against MSW mocks, the backend's nine bulk-built modules have never served the real UI, auth is a dev stub on both sides, and neither repo is under version control. The client's next commercial ask is end-to-end tenant management/onboarding, with a governed Platform Control Center behind it. The plan therefore runs: protect & integrate (N1) → real auth & staging (N2) → tenant onboarding (N3–N4 = TEN T1/T2) → control center + hardening (N5), with ABDM as a parallel workstream from N2 because its 3–6 month clock must start now. Cadence stays 2-week sprints; both teams work the same sprint on coupled tickets.
Sprint N1 cannot start on unversioned code.
Day 1, hour 1 of N1 is git init/commit/push + branch protection on BOTH repos (ticket N1-00). Integration means bisecting, reverting, and diffing — impossible without history. No other ticket may merge before N1-00 is done.

Parallel Workstream P — ABDM (starts in N2, runs through N5+)
ABDM is 3–6 months of calendar time driven by external certification; it cannot wait for a sprint slot. One engineer (rotating pair) owns it continuously: P-1 sandbox registration + credentials (N2); P-2 ABHA create/verify at registration wired into REG + the registration UI's ABHA field (N3); P-3 HFR facility + HPR provider linkage in tenant onboarding (N4 — lands naturally inside the Setup Wizard as an 'ABDM' step for Indian tenants); P-4 FHIR conformance for M1 records + sandbox milestone testing (N5). Certification submission targets end of N5; production ABDM follows in the release train after.
